GOOGLE ANALYTICS 4 SETUP
========================

Your site has the GA4 tracking code added to index.html but it uses
a placeholder ID (G-XXXXXXXXXX). You need to replace it with your
real Measurement ID.

STEPS:
------

1. Go to https://analytics.google.com
2. Click "Start measuring" (or sign in if you have an account)
3. Create an Account name (e.g. "TKDigital")
4. Create a Property name (e.g. "TKDigital Website")
5. Under "Data Streams", click "Web"
6. Enter your website URL: https://tkdigital.media
7. Enter a stream name: "TKDigital Main Site"
8. Click "Create stream"
9. Copy your Measurement ID — it looks like G-XXXXXXXXXX

THEN:
-----

Open index.html and find these two lines (near the top of <head>):

   src="https://www.googletagmanager.com/gtag/js?id=G-XXXXXXXXXX"
   gtag('config', 'G-XXXXXXXXXX');

Replace G-XXXXXXXXXX in BOTH places with your real Measurement ID.

ALSO SET UP GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE:
-----------------------------------

1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Add property: https://tkdigital.media
3. Verify ownership (easiest: use your Google Analytics verification)
4. Submit your sitemap: https://tkdigital.media/sitemap.xml
5. This lets Google index your site and you can monitor search performance
